🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Identify places in a neighbourhood context.
  • Understand functions of linking words: AND (addition), BUT (contrast), SO (result).
  • Construct meaningful compound sentences about neighbourhoods verbally and in notebooks.

⏱️ 70-Minute JP Breakdown

JP 1 (35 Min): Context (7m) → Speaking (8m) → Reading Anita & Blanca (20m)
JP 2 (35 Min): Reading Check (5m) → Grammar Detective & Discovery (15m) → Guided Practice & Game (10m) → Production & Exit (5m)
